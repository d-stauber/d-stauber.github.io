How to Play Tolerance

Goal: Be the first player to reach 10 Victory Points.

Setup: Each player places 2 clinics and 2 pathways on the board. The second clinic grants starting resources from adjacent tiles.

On your turn:

  1. Roll the dice. All players collect resources from tiles matching the number (except the flare tile).
  2. If a 7 is rolled, the flare moves. Players with 8+ cards must discard half.
  3. Build pathways (1 TC + 1 MY), clinics (1 BC + 1 TC + 1 MY + 1 TL), or research centers (3 CY + 2 BC). You can also buy or play therapy cards.
  4. End your turn.

Victory Points: 1 VP per clinic · 2 VP per research center · 2 VP for Strongest Pathway · 2 VP for Most Care · 1–2 VP from hidden therapy cards.

Therapy Cards: Anti-Cytokine moves the flare and steals a resource. Treg Boost gives 2 free resources. Plasmapheresis takes all of one resource from opponents. Costimulation Blockade gives 2 free pathways. JAK Inhibition gives 2 free pathways. Precision Breakthrough is worth 1 hidden VP.

Trade: Trade 4 of any resource for 1 of any other with the bank. Build a clinic or research center on a port (⚕) to unlock better rates:

  • Treatment Center — 3:1 any resource (build on any ⚕ circle on the board edge)
  • Cytokine Lab / B-Cell Center / T-Cell Center / Myeloid Hub / Tolerance Institute — 2:1 for that specific resource

The Flare: The flare tile produces nothing. When it moves, the player who rolled may steal 1 resource from a bordering opponent.
