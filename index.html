How to Play
Tolerance — A Strategy Game About Autoimmunity
Objective

You are a physician-researcher assigned two patients, each with a different autoimmune disease. Be the first to achieve durable immune tolerance in both — not by erasing disease, but by bringing the immune system to lasting stability.

On Your Turn
1
Roll the dice
Every board tile has a number. When you roll it, all players with a clinic or research center on that tile's corners collect the matching immune insight.
2
Build your network (optional)
Spend immune insight to expand your clinical infrastructure:
Pathway
CY + AR
Clinic
CY + BC + TC + MY
Research Center
BC + TC + MY + AR×2
3
Draw a therapy card (optional)
Draw from one of four specialist decks. Each costs 3 of that deck's insight + 1 each of the other three (6 total). You can also exchange an unwanted card back to its deck for 1 of that insight (once per turn).
4
Treat a patient (when ready)
If you hold a therapy card that matches one of your diseases, click Apply Therapy in your disease panel. Treat both patients to win.
Therapy Decks
Cytokine (CY)
Anti-TNF, anti-IL-6, anti-IL-17, anti-IFN therapies
B Cell (BC)
Anti-CD20, anti-BAFF, FcRn antagonists
T Cell (TC)
Co-stimulation blockade, S1P modulators, gut-homing blockers
Antigen Presentation (AP)
Anti-GM-CSF, TLR inhibitors, tolerogenic dendritic cell strategies
Immune Insight Types
CY Cytokine BC B Cell TC T Cell AP Antigen Presentation AR Advanced Research
Click any insight tile or legend item in the game to learn what it represents biologically.
Budget Cuts (rolling 7)

Rolling a 7 triggers a budget cut. Any researcher holding more than 9 insight cards must return half. The budget cut marker then moves to any tile of your choice — that tile produces nothing until the marker moves again.

Ports & Trading

Coastal ports let you trade insight with the bank at better rates. Specialist ports (2:1) trade one insight type at half cost. General ports (3:1) accept any insight type. Build a pathway and clinic to reach a port and unlock its rate.
